# Source Index — Resume Research Corpus

This document lists all primary, secondary, and tertiary evidence sources cited in `resume_research.md`. Each source is categorized by type, confidence level, and relevance to early-career AI, software, voice, wearable, and startup engineering resumes.

## Tier 1 Sources (High Confidence: Academic Institutions, Official Hiring Guides, Verified Founders)

### 1. MIT CAPD Career Handbook & Action Verbs Guide
- **Author / Organization**: MIT Career Advising & Professional Development (CAPD)
- **Source Type**: Official University Career Guide
- **Date**: 2024 / Continuous Update
- **URL**: https://capd.mit.edu/resources/career-handbook/
- **Why it matters**: Authoritative baseline for technical bullet construction, action verb categorization, concise single-page formatting, and quantifying engineering achievements.
- **Confidence Level**: **HIGH**

### 2. Harvard OCS Resume & Cover Letter Guide
- **Author / Organization**: Harvard Office of Career Services (OCS)
- **Source Type**: Official University Career Guide
- **Date**: 2023 / 2024
- **URL**: https://ocs.fas.harvard.edu/resumes-cvs

- **Confidence Level**: **HIGH**
